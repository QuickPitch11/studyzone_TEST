Calculus Basics

Study of change (derivatives) and accumulation (integrals)

What is Calculus?

Calculus is the branch of mathematics that studies change and motion (differential calculus) and accumulation and area (integral calculus). It introduces limits, derivatives, integrals, and the fundamental theorem connecting them.

Core Topics

  • • Limits: Foundation for continuity, derivatives, and integrals.
  • • Derivatives: Rates of change and slopes of tangent lines.
  • • Integrals: Accumulation, area under curves, and antiderivatives.
  • • Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: Connects differentiation and integration.

Applications

Calculus is essential in physics, engineering, economics, biology, and any field involving change or accumulation — e.g., motion, optimization, growth models, and computing areas/volumes.
