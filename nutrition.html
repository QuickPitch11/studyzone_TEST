Nutrition Basics

Study of food and its effects on health

What is Nutrition?

Nutrition is the science that studies how food and nutrients affect the body's health, growth, and maintenance. It encompasses the processes by which organisms take in and utilize food substances for energy, growth, and maintaining bodily functions.

Essential Nutrients

  • • Macronutrients: Carbohydrates, proteins, and fats - required in large amounts for energy and body functions
  • • Micronutrients: Vitamins and minerals - required in smaller amounts but essential for health
  • • Water: Essential for all bodily functions, making up about 60% of body weight
  • • Fiber: Important for digestive health, though not directly absorbed by the body

Major Nutrient Categories

  1. 1. Carbohydrates: Primary energy source (4 calories/gram). Found in grains, fruits, vegetables, and legumes
  2. 2. Proteins: Building blocks for tissues, enzymes, and hormones (4 calories/gram). Found in meat, dairy, legumes, and nuts
  3. 3. Fats: Energy storage, vitamin absorption, cell membrane structure (9 calories/gram). Includes saturated, unsaturated, and trans fats
  4. 4. Vitamins: Organic compounds essential for metabolism, immunity, and growth. Water-soluble (B, C) and fat-soluble (A, D, E, K)
  5. 5. Minerals: Inorganic elements needed for bone structure, fluid balance, and enzyme function (calcium, iron, zinc, etc.)

Key Principles

A balanced diet provides all essential nutrients in appropriate amounts. Dietary guidelines emphasize variety, moderation, and proportion. Nutrition plays a crucial role in preventing chronic diseases, supporting growth and development, and maintaining optimal health throughout the lifespan. Understanding nutrition helps individuals make informed food choices that support their health goals.
