Biology Basics

Study of living organisms

What is Biology?

Biology is the scientific study of life and living organisms, including their structure, function, growth, evolution, distribution, and taxonomy. It encompasses everything from microscopic cells to entire ecosystems.

Core Principles

  • • Cell Theory: All living things are composed of cells
  • • Evolution: Species change over time through natural selection
  • • Genetics: Traits are inherited through DNA
  • • Homeostasis: Organisms maintain internal stability
  • • Energy Flow: Organisms require and transfer energy

Major Branches

  1. 1. Molecular Biology: Study of biological molecules and processes
  2. 2. Cell Biology: Structure and function of cells
  3. 3. Genetics: Heredity and variation in organisms
  4. 4. Ecology: Interactions between organisms and their environment
  5. 5. Physiology: Functions and processes of living systems

Key Concepts

DNA and RNA carry genetic information. Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy. Cellular respiration releases energy from food. Organisms are classified into domains, kingdoms, and species. Ecosystems involve complex food webs and nutrient cycles.
