Research Methods Basics

Systematic approaches to investigation and knowledge creation

What are Research Methods?

Research methods are systematic techniques and procedures used to collect, analyze, and interpret data to answer research questions or test hypotheses. They provide a structured framework for conducting scientific investigations and generating reliable, valid knowledge across disciplines.

Key Concepts

  • • Research Design: The overall plan and structure for conducting a study
  • • Data Collection: Gathering information through surveys, experiments, interviews, or observations
  • • Validity: The accuracy and truthfulness of research findings
  • • Reliability: The consistency and reproducibility of research results

Major Approaches

  1. 1. Quantitative Research: Numerical data analysis using statistical methods to test hypotheses
  2. 2. Qualitative Research: In-depth exploration of meanings, experiences, and perspectives through non-numerical data
  3. 3. Mixed Methods: Combining quantitative and qualitative approaches for comprehensive understanding
  4. 4. Experimental Research: Manipulating variables to establish cause-and-effect relationships
  5. 5. Observational Research: Studying phenomena in natural settings without intervention

Research Process

The research process typically follows these steps: formulating research questions, reviewing existing literature, selecting appropriate methodology, collecting and analyzing data, drawing conclusions, and reporting findings. Ethical considerations, including informed consent and data privacy, are essential throughout the process.
