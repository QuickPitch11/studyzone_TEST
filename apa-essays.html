APA Essays Basics

Formatting and structure for academic writing

What are APA Essays?

APA (American Psychological Association) format is a standardized writing style used primarily in the social sciences, education, and psychology. It provides guidelines for formatting papers, citing sources, and organizing content to ensure clarity, credibility, and consistency in academic writing.

Key Formatting Rules

  • • Margins: 1 inch on all sides
  • • Font: Recommended fonts include Times New Roman (12pt), Calibri (11pt), or Arial (11pt)
  • • Spacing: Double-space throughout the entire paper, including references
  • • Page Numbers: Top-right corner of every page

Paper Structure

  1. 1. Title Page: Includes paper title, author name, institutional affiliation, course information, instructor name, and due date
  2. 2. Abstract: Brief summary (150-250 words) on a separate page
  3. 3. Main Body: Introduction, method, results, and discussion sections (varies by paper type)
  4. 4. References: Alphabetical list of all cited sources on a new page with hanging indent
  5. 5. Appendices (if needed): Supplementary materials like surveys, tables, or detailed data

Citations and References

APA uses author-date citation format. In-text citations include the author's last name and publication year (Smith, 2023). Direct quotes also include page numbers (Smith, 2023, p. 45). The References page lists full bibliographic information for all cited sources, organized alphabetically with hanging indents.
